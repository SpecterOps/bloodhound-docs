---
title: EntraDSFor
description: Correlates an Azure Microsoft Entra Domain Services resource to its managed AD Domain.
---

<img noZoom src="/assets/enterprise-AND-community-edition-pill-tag.svg" alt="Applies to BloodHound Enterprise and CE"/>

`EntraDSFor` is a non-traversable, post-processed correlation. BloodHound requires a unique normalized match between `AZEntraDS.domainname` and `Domain.name`, then corroborates the domain SID through the tenant's synchronized `AAD DC Administrators` group.

The group must be represented by an AZGroup named `AAD DC Administrators`, a `SyncedToEntraDSGroup` relationship to its Entra DS Group, and a matching `domainsid` on the candidate Domain. BloodHound fails closed when the name match is missing or ambiguous or the group correlation does not corroborate the SID.

## Edge Schema

Source: [AZEntraDS](/resources/nodes/az-entra-ds)<br />
Destination: [Domain](/resources/nodes/domain)<br />
Traversable: **No**
