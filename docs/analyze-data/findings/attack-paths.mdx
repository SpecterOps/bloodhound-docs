The **Attack Paths** page in BloodHound Enterprise gives you a high-level overview of where identity risk exists, how much of your environment is exposed, and which areas need attention first.

These risks are represented as findings. Findings include exposure and impact metrics that quantify risk, so you can assess overall risk in one place and then use those metrics to prioritize remediation as you drill into specific finding types.

The **Attack Paths** page has two views:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Graph view (default)" icon="diagram-project" href="/analyze-data/findings/graph-view">
    Understand how risk concentrates in a selected environment and zone, view exposure and impact metrics, and expand finding details for remediation guidance.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Table view (beta)" icon="table" href="/analyze-data/findings/table-view">
    Triage many findings at once, compare findings across environments and zones, and preserve filter and sort context in the page URL.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Findings are organized by the environment of the target nodes, not the environment where the finding is defined.
  
  For example, an OpenGraph extension can define a finding that targets Active Directory nodes. That finding appears under the relevant Active Directory domain, not the OpenGraph environment.
</Note>

## Choose a view

Use the view that matches your investigation goal.

| Goal | Recommended view |
| --- | --- |
| Understand where risk concentrates in one environment and zone | **Graph view** |
| Review risk metrics and remediation guidance for a specific finding type | **Graph view** |
| Triage many findings across environments, platforms, zones, and statuses | **Table view** |
| Share a filtered and sorted findings list with a URL | **Table view** |
