Privilege Zones can support time-boxed remediation work. Use this approach when your team needs to focus on one audit finding, executive priority, migration, application environment, or sensitive area where risk reduction needs to be visible quickly.

A campaign-focused Privilege Zone creates a clear scope for the work. BloodHound then helps you identify unexpected attack paths, prioritize remediation, assign owners, and measure progress over time.

## Structure the campaign

Use a zone to define the protected boundary for the campaign. Use labels to organize supporting details such as owner, workstream, exception status, or migration phase.

| Campaign activity | Privilege Zones support |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Define scope | Model the protected assets as a zone. |
| Identify exposure | Review attack paths that cross into the zone. |
| Assign work | Use labels or reporting workflows to align findings with owners. |
| Measure progress | Track changes in Attack Paths and Posture as remediation work lands. |
| Close or continue | Decide whether the zone remains for ongoing monitoring after the campaign ends. |

## Lower the barrier to action

You do not need a complete enterprise privilege model before you use Privilege Zones. A focused campaign gives your team a smaller, measurable starting point. As remediation progresses, you can refine the zone and decide whether the campaign boundary becomes part of your long-term operating model.

## Use this approach when

- You need a bounded remediation effort with visible progress.
- You are responding to an audit or compliance concern.
- You want to reduce exposure before a migration or restructuring.
- You need to coordinate remediation across multiple teams.

## Move from concept to configuration

Create a [zone](/analyze-data/privilege-zones/zones) for the campaign boundary. Use [labels](/analyze-data/privilege-zones/labels) to support assignment and tracking. Review [Attack Paths](/analyze-data/findings/attack-paths) and [Posture](/analyze-data/findings/posture) to measure campaign progress.
