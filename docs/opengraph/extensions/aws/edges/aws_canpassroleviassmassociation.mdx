Observed SSMAssociation service route contributing to AWS_CanPassRoleToService. This evidence edge is not independently traversable.

## Edge schema

| Source kind | Destination kind |
| --- | --- |
| [AWS_Role](/opengraph/extensions/aws/nodes/aws_role) | [AWS_Role](/opengraph/extensions/aws/nodes/aws_role) |
| [AWS_User](/opengraph/extensions/aws/nodes/aws_user) | [AWS_Role](/opengraph/extensions/aws/nodes/aws_role) |

**Traversable:** No. This relationship supplies structural or permission evidence and does not independently form an Attack Path step.

## Service route requirements

The principal needs `iam:PassRole` on the target role and one complete operation bundle below in the same account. The destination is the passed role, rather than the service resource used to authorize the operation.

| Trusted service | Required operation bundle | Trust rewrite supported |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ssm.amazonaws.com` | `ssm:CreateDocument` + `ssm:CreateAssociation` | Yes |

The role must trust the listed service. Where trust rewriting is supported, `iam:UpdateAssumeRolePolicy` on that role can supply an alternative. The collector checks `iam:PassedToService` conditions and records the selected route in `methods`. This evidence contributes to [AWS_CanPassRoleToService](/opengraph/extensions/aws/edges/aws_canpassroletoservice).

## Evaluation limits

This edge represents the collector's model of collected authorization and resource state. It does not prove that every runtime request succeeds. Inspect unresolved `conditions` and `trust_policy_conditions` when present; `has_conditions` and `has_trust_policy_conditions` indicate retained evidence. Composite edges can include `methods` or `composite_components` describing their derivation.
